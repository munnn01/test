## OT: align a later guard probe with HOTA

The [current causal guard](../adaptive_vcm/v34r1/tracking.py) already clones committed tracker state and commits once. Its cost is `(misses + FP + 2 × switches)/source detections` at IoU `.5`. It is not HOTA: the authors' metric uses trajectory association Jaccard statistics and combines DetA and AssA across localization thresholds `.05:.05:.95`. [HOTA paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-020-01375-2), [official implementation](https://github.com/JonathonLuiten/TrackEval/blob/master/trackeval/metrics/hota.py).

After the OD probe, compare that guard with **source-track pseudo-HOTA on the observed sequence prefix**, plus separate DetA/AssA constraints, on predeclared DEV sequences. Every trial must use identical committed decoded history and source history; compare candidate and identity trials on that same prefix; reset at sequence boundaries. Prefix scoring is causal but can dilute recent harm, so also log current-window misses and boundary switches. A fixed trailing-window variant is a second hypothesis if prefix dilution is observed. Neither is full-sequence GT HOTA or a bound on future association errors.

Separately test masks that preserve low-score detections associated with existing source tracklets, using the actual tracker thresholds. ByteTrack recovers occluded objects by associating low-score boxes with unmatched tracklets; it does not justify protecting every low-score background box. Report whole-sequence GT HOTA/DetA/AssA and boundary failures. [ByteTrack paper, §3](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136820001.pdf), [author code](https://github.com/FoundationVision/ByteTrack).

## Larger alternatives and transfer limits

| Alternative | What the primary evidence supports; work beyond A3 |
|---|---|
| **Trained QP-conditioned RGB preprocessor** | Lu et al. train spatial preprocessing with QP-conditioned feature scaling, frozen downstream models, a real BPG forward path and learned codec proxy for backpropagation. Their setup uses BPG QP28/31/34/37/41 and three substantial training stages; it is materially different from selecting fixed filters. The author repository provides both fixed-QP and quantization-adaptive modules. An H.264/H.265 study must train/validate its codec-conditioned surrogate and RGB network on TRAIN, measure real bytes on DEV, and separately assess temporal behavior. Image-detection/classification findings do not establish AR or HOTA gains. [Paper, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.05650), [author code](https://github.com/XingtongGe/PreprocessingICM). |
| **RoI geometry retargeting** | Różek et al. reduce the coded frame geometry, transmit RoI metadata and inverse-retarget at the decoder; their implementation uses VVC/VCM-RS. This changes the current geometry-preserving contract. A separate branch must pay metadata bytes, reconstruct canonical geometry/coordinates, and define latency and temporal grids. The paper's VVC results and CTC monotonic curve fitting cannot establish gains under this project's H.264/H.265 four-point PCHIP protocol. Source availability is limited to MPEG experts; the public author contribution documents the syntax and component names. [Paper, §§4–6 and data availability](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13640-025-00682-3), [author contribution m62235](https://www.multimedia.edu.pl/publications/m62235-VCM-CE16---RoI-based-simplification-and-retargeting-for-Video--Coding-for-Machines.pdf). |
