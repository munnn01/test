# A5: phản biện AutoResearchClaw qua ACP

Phản hồi thật từ AutoResearchClaw ACPClient → acpx → Codex đã đăng nhập, chỉ gửi tóm tắt thiết kế, không gửi mã nguồn/media/khóa. Ba lần khởi động đầu lỗi vì bridge ghi đè CODEX_PATH bằng đường dẫn app cũ; đã sửa để tôn trọng executable hợp lệ và dùng fallback đã cài. Ba kiểm thử subprocess giả được quan sát RED rồi GREEN. Phản hồi dưới đây nhận sau khi job OT A5 đã gửi lên Kaggle; không thay lambda, FIT rule hay cấu hình sau khi nhận phản hồi. Đây là phản biện có giới hạn, không phải chạy đủ26 giai đoạn nghiên cứu.

1. Objective mismatch. Summing per-window log byte ratios can favor different actions from minimizing aggregate paid bytes; small and large windows receive comparable influence. The paid≤identity restriction also excludes locally expensive actions that might improve later tracking. Specify the source-HOTA cost’s sign and scale, and report aggregate bytes alongside the selection surrogate. Endpoint-scaled utility cannot establish the target: compute BD-rate from exactly QP30/35/40/45 and leave nonmonotone curves undefined.

2. Selection uncertainty. Seven lambdas on two short FIT sequences remain vulnerable to sequence-specific selection. Fully specify the GT HOTA-drop utility, endpoint scaling, codec/QP aggregation, and identity fallback before replay. Preserve per-sequence outcomes and lambda sensitivity. ReusedCAL can assess transfer within this research setting, but cannot independently confirm success; OT findings also cannot establish AR/OD performance.

3. Stateful replay validity. Cached detections support alternate policies only if every required candidate/window is covered and detector outputs are independent of tracker history. Recompute candidate tracker transitions from each lambda’s own committed past; previously cached tracker scores cannot substitute. Define trailing-history warm-up, sequence resets, and codec-window boundary behavior. Require byte-exact identity-anchor reproduction against A3 and consistent charging of every paid header.

Cheap discriminating diagnostic: On fixed A3 common-past cache snapshots, plot log paid-byte ratio against identity-relative source-HOTA cost. Mark the old hard-gate exclusions and each lambda’s winner. Count newly selected tradeoffs and calculate their byte-weighted savings. This distinguishes useful opportunities unlocked by removing gates from a bank offering little additional tradeoff; it does not establish replayed GT gains.
