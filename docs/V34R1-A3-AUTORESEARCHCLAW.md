1. Source proxies can reward detector self-consistency while preserving errors. Source pseudoAP ignores missed objects and inherits false positives; pseudo-target losses may favor overconfident predictions. Check proxy changes against GT AP changes on FIT, including class, object-size and baseline-confidence strata. Report cases where accepted filtering reduces GT AP, and compare each proxy against the existing guard and same-QP decoded identity. Identical RNG seeds do not guarantee identical proposals: distinguish fixed-proposal loss comparisons from changes caused by proposal generation, and verify deterministic inference without model-state updates.

2. Selection uncertainty and cohort dependence may dominate the expected gains. Searching thresholds across 21 profiles can exploit noise in 64 FIT images; correlated frames or shared scenes can make nominally disjoint partitions dependent. Check image, video, scene and near-duplicate overlap against historical exposure and across splits. Preregister the complete threshold grid, tie-breaking, AP scale, aggregation across codec/QP conditions and identity fallback. Apply AP constraints using full-cohort official COCO evaluation for each condition. Freeze the policy and implementation before validation; report paired, cluster-aware uncertainty for AP differences and rate savings. A validation failure remains a failure without retuning.

3. End-to-end accounting and BD-rate validity can invalidate otherwise favorable results. Audit all 21,504 logical rows, explicitly identifying cached or reused identities. Count actual transmitted bytes, including required signaling and headers; independently decode representative packets and verify dimensions, frame counts, timestamps and hashes. Repeated runs should reproduce bytes and predictions, with pinned codec builds, detector settings and proposal handling. Define the BD-rate method, integration direction and common quality interval beforehand; retain every raw point and declare unsupported, invalid or nonmonotone curves undefined. Minimized paid bytes and hard rate caps do not establish a quality advantage. This OD diagnosis also provides no direct evidence for action recognition or tracking: those require separate frozen-model evaluations with temporal integrity preserved. None of these checks guarantees strictly below −15% BD-rate.
